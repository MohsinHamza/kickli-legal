Important: This page is a draft. The email link below is a request channel, not an automated deletion system. Before publishing, confirm that the team can verify requests, delete the account and associated data from the backend, handle device-local data, and communicate completion.

How to request deletion

  1. Email pixeltronixlab@gmail.com with the subject Kickli account and data deletion.
  2. Send the request from the email address associated with your Kickli account, if applicable. If you cannot access that address, explain this in your message. Do not email passwords or authentication codes.
  3. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account. We will not ask you to send your password.
  4. We will confirm receipt and, after verification, process the request according to the retention rules explained below.

Email Kickli to request account deletion

What deletion should cover

When a verified request is approved, Kickli should delete the account and associated personal data that it is not required or permitted to retain. This may include cloud-stored kick-session history, profile/account data, invitation or sharing records, and other data linked to the account, if those categories are collected by the released app.

Verify before publishing: document exactly what is deleted, whether shared copies or data already seen by invited partners can be removed, whether purchase records are retained by an app-store provider, and how backups are handled. Do not promise deletion of data that Kickli cannot control.

Data that may be retained

Verify before publishing: if any information must be retained for legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution reasons, identify the categories, reason, and retention period here. If no such retention applies, say so only after confirming it.

What about data stored on your phone?

Verify before publishing: explain whether signing out, deleting the account, or uninstalling Kickli removes local session history. If local records are not automatically removed by account deletion, give clear steps for deleting them.

How long will it take?

Verify before publishing: insert the actual processing target and any different timeline for backups or data that must be retained. Do not promise a timeframe the team cannot meet.

Need help?
