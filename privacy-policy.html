1. About Kickli

Kickli is a baby kick-counting app that lets users record movement sessions and look back at past sessions. Depending on the features used, users may also share a session journey with invited partners or family members and access paid Pro features.

2. Information we may process

Kickli supports account sign-up and login, and session records are stored on the device and in a cloud service. The exact data fields must be confirmed before publication.

  • Account information — Verify before publishing: Identify whether the app/backend stores an email address, display name, authentication identifier, profile details, or other sign-in information.
  • Kick session information — Verify before publishing: Identify the precise fields stored, such as session timestamps, movement counts, session duration, history, notes, or generated reports. Do not include fields that the app does not collect.
  • Partner/family sharing — Verify before publishing: Confirm whether invitation codes, relationship/member details, or shared session data are stored and who can access them.
  • Purchases and subscriptions — Verify before publishing: Confirm what purchase entitlement or transaction-related information the app or its providers receive for Pro plans. Do not claim Kickli receives payment-card details unless this is true.
  • Diagnostics and usage data — Verify before publishing: Identify the analytics/crash-reporting SDK(s) and their actual collection, such as crash traces, device/app information, diagnostics, events, identifiers, or performance data.

3. How information is used

Describe only the purposes that apply in the released app. Potential purposes to verify include providing sign-in, saving and syncing sessions, displaying history, enabling user-authorized sharing, supporting subscriptions, diagnosing crashes, improving reliability, responding to support requests, and meeting legal obligations.

4. Storage, retention, and security

Kickli session records are stored on the device and in a cloud service. Verify before publishing: name the backend/storage provider; explain what is stored locally versus remotely; state how long records are retained; describe the deletion and backup-retention process; and confirm the safeguards actually implemented. 6. Children and sensitive context

Kickli is designed for adults to record pregnancy-related movement sessions. Do not enter a baby's name or other identifying information unless the app asks for it and the policy explains its handling. Verify before publishing: confirm the intended user age, Google Play target-audience settings, and whether any information could be treated as health-related or otherwise sensitive under applicable law or platform rules.
